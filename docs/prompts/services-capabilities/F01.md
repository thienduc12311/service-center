# F01 — Scoped leadership, private visibility and hierarchy

Read [execution rules](../IMPLEMENTATION-RULES.md). **User story:** A team leader manages their ministry without organization-wide power, and members cannot discover prepared requests or restricted fields/files.

**Read:** Services spec F01; US-01.1/01.2/01.3; backend auth/organization middleware and resource routes; current RLS, people projections, Storage policies, session/context responses; shared role helpers; web Teams/People/Plans/Settings surfaces. Inspect MOB-005 Pass A/D before adding overlapping foundations.

## Pass A — Effective capabilities

1. Inventory current coarse roles and every privileged/data-output path. Define capabilities for directory/private people fields, plan/scheduling, songs/library and media, scoped to team/service type where appropriate. Preserve owner/admin and current scheduler behavior during compatible rollout; a team grant cannot issue administrator grants. **Done:** an actor/resource/action matrix explains effective access, deny/ceiling rules and grant administration.
2. Persist grants and centralize caller/capability resolution used by API, SQL/RLS, Storage and workers. Check same-org scope and prevent privilege escalation through updates/moves. Add effective-access context/detail contracts and admin review/edit UI. **Done:** a Band-only leader cannot mutate Hosts through REST or direct DB calls and can see why access is allowed/denied.

## Pass B — Audience and response ownership

3. Enforce prepared/released visibility independently from draft/published and notified state. Audit lists/counts/joins, search, calendars/feeds, reports, token previews and file issuance, especially service-role queries. Preserve existing visible assignments with an explicit migration policy. **Done:** prepared fixtures are undiscoverable to their recipient across all output paths.
4. Add explicit response owner defaulting to the scheduling actor, with scoped reassignment/audit and consistent reply/update routing. Protect sensitive Person details through actual projections/policies. **Done:** public/signed-in response effects reach the authorized owner without leaking private reasons to general audiences.

## Pass C — Separately selected extensions

5. Add nested folders, ordering/landing choice and inherited grants only through cycle-safe, revisioned moves and effective-access preview. Archive/copy service types without copying people/grants implicitly; share teams through explicit service-type associations. **Done:** hierarchy changes cannot cross tenants or secretly broaden access, and historical plans remain readable by their intended audience.
6. Add team defaults/tag-derived membership/related-team visibility/secure eligibility one policy at a time. Secure eligibility uses explicit current records/provider evidence and fails closed when missing/expired; do not build a background-check product. **Done:** each enabled policy has direct-access, revocation and inheritance tests.

**Acceptance:** API/RLS/Storage agreement, preserved legacy role behavior, field/private preparation safety and tested revocation. Extensions are separately tracked and never prerequisites for simple scoped leadership.

**Handoff:** capability matrix, migration compatibility, covered output channels, selected extensions and tests. Mobile consumes these contracts in MOB-005/008/012/031.
