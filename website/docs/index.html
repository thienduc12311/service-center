Overview

Service Center helps churches plan worship services and schedule the volunteer teams who serve in them.

A church (an organization) runs recurring services. Each service has a plan: its date and times, the order of service (songs, headers and other items), and the people scheduled to serve. People belong to teams such as Band, Production or Hospitality, and can serve in a position on that team, such as Bass or Sound. Each time someone is asked to serve, they get an assignment they can accept or decline.

I'm a volunteerSee when you serve, respond to requests, block out dates and prepare. I lead a team or plan servicesBuild plans, schedule people, manage songs and run the service live. I'm setting up a churchRegister your church, invite people and set up teams. I'm using the mobile appTabs, widgets, offline downloads and push notifications.

Key words

TermMeaning
PlanOne service: its date, times, order of service and who is scheduled.
Service typeA recurring kind of service with its own default teams, like "Sunday Morning" or "Youth Night".
Plan itemOne entry in the order of service: a song, a section header or any other item.
RehearsalA preparation time attached to a plan, separate from the service itself.
Team / PositionA group people are scheduled into (Band) and an optional role within it (Bass).
AssignmentA request for a person to serve on a plan. It is Pending until they reply, then Confirmed or Declined.
BlockoutA date or time someone is unavailable.

Getting started

Create an account, then join your church or register a new one.

Create an account or sign in WebiOS

Sign-in screen
  1. Open the web app or the mobile app and choose Create account.
  2. Enter your email and a password.
  3. Once you're signed in, the Get started screen helps you join a church or register a new one.

Forgot your password? Choose Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. We'll email you a reset link that works for one hour. On mobile, the link opens on the website.

Accept an invitation WebiOS

If a church owner invites you, you'll get an email. Open the link, set a password and you're in, with the role you were invited with. On a phone with the app installed, the link opens the app.

Join an existing church WebiOS

  1. On Get started, choose Join an existing church.
  2. Enter the Church ID (for example riverside), or paste a link your church shared.
  3. Confirm it's the right church, add a short message and choose Send request.
  4. The church owner approves it in People → Join requests, and you'll get an email invitation.

You can withdraw a request while it's waiting.

Register a new church WebiOS

  1. On Get started, choose Register a new church.
  2. Fill in the church name, leader, phone, address, size and time zone, then choose Send for review.
  3. While it's pending you can edit or withdraw the application. When it's approved, you become the church owner.

Belong to more than one church

Switch churches from the church menu at the top of the web sidebar, or in the app from Settings → Switch church. To register another church, go to Settings → Churches.

First steps for a new church owner Web

  1. Add your church's details and logo in Settings → Church.
  2. Create a service type with the wizard. It sets up your recurring schedule and common teams.
  3. Add people, or import them from a CSV, and invite them.
  4. Add your songs, then create your first plan.

Roles & permissions

Every member has one role. Owners and admins can add extra permissions for a whole church, a folder, a service type or a single team.

RoleWhat they can do
OwnerEverything. Only owners manage People (add records, invite, change roles, bulk edits, join requests, CSV import) and see everyone's contact details.
AdminEverything except owner-only People management, including access settings, delivery status, integrations and church settings.
SchedulerSchedules people, prepares and releases requests, sends reminders, and sees blockouts and decline reasons. Can be church-wide or limited to only granted areas.
MemberSees their own schedule, responds, sets availability, rehearses, chats, and reads the plans and files shared with them.

Extra permissions

Owners and admins can grant any of these, for the whole church or a specific folder, service type or team:

  • Edit plans: change the order of service, times, notes and templates.
  • Schedule people: send serving requests.
  • Schedule over conflicts: schedule someone despite a blockout or double-booking (a reason is recorded).
  • Manage teams: makes someone a team leader for that team's roster and settings.
  • Manage songs, Manage media, Manage people and See private person details.

In the app, leader means anyone who can edit plans, schedule people or manage a team somewhere. Leaders see Reports and leader-only notes and files. Use Settings → Access → Check access to see why someone can or can't do something.

Other kinds of people

  • Helper: someone you trust to answer requests or manage availability for you. See Help with my schedule.
  • Guest: someone on the roster who has no login. They can be scheduled and answer from the email link.

My schedule & responding

See every service you're on and answer serving requests in one tap.

Dashboard Web

Home page of the web app
  • Waiting on you: requests you haven't answered, with one-click Accept.
  • Upcoming services: with confirmed and pending counts.
  • Your next commitments: including rehearsal times.

My schedule WebiOS

Web: My schedule · App: Schedule tab
  • Every plan you're on, with each position you're serving in.
  • Accept or Decline (with an optional reason). You can Withdraw later if plans change.
  • Review pending responses lets you answer several requests at once, per position and per service time.
  • Switch between Upcoming and History, and filter by Pending, Confirmed or Declined.
  • In the app, jump to Open positions and Replacements from here.

Respond from email without signing in WebiOS

Every request email has Accept and Decline links. They work without a password and only change the positions and times shown in that email.

Search Web

Press ⌘ K (Mac) or Ctrl K to search people, teams, positions, plans, songs and files.

Availability & blockouts

Tell your schedulers when you can't serve, so they know before they ask.

Blockout dates WebiOS

Settings → Availability
  • Block out a single date, or a repeating weekly or monthly series.
  • All-day or a specific time range, with a private reason only schedulers can see.
  • The app adds, edits and removes one-off blockouts. Manage repeating series on the website.

Availability review Web

When a team leader asks for an availability update, you'll be asked to confirm your dates by a reply-by date. If nothing has changed, just confirm.

Scheduling preferences WebiOS

For each team, or all teams at once, set how often you'd like to serve, a maximum per month, preferred or avoided weeks of the month, preferred start times and dates to avoid. Schedulers see these as gentle warnings, not hard rules.

Help with my schedule (helpers) WebiOS

Let a trusted person, like a spouse or parent, see your requests, answer requests for you, or manage your availability, optionally until a set date. Helpers never see plan notes or files. In the app, a household picker lets helpers switch between the people they help.

Across my churches WebiOS

If you serve at more than one church, opt in to see a combined schedule, let other churches' schedulers see that you're busy, and copy blockouts to another church.

Songs & chord charts

A shared song library with arrangements, keys and readable chord charts.

Song library WebiOS

Web: Songs · App: Songs tab
  • Search and filter by key, theme, type, style, speed, author, BPM and last-scheduled date. Show active, archived or all songs.
  • Add a song with its details, and paste a chart as chords-over-lyrics or ChordPro ([G]Amazing grace).
  • Web Edit many songs at once, import songs from a CSV file, merge duplicates and delete songs.
  • Adding and changing songs needs the Manage songs permission.

Song page & arrangements WebiOS

  • Arrangements: each has its own key, BPM, meter, capo, length, sequence and chart. Mark one as the default.
  • Keys: save labeled keys (sounding key, capo and a vocal label such as "Female") with their own files.
  • Duplicate arrangement to start a new version, and archive or restore songs and arrangements.
  • See the song's schedule history: when and where it was last used. Attach files to the song, an arrangement or a key.

Viewing charts WebiOS

Charts inside a plan show automatically in that service's key. Choose how chords appear:

NotationExample
ChordsG · C · D
Numbers (Nashville)1 · 4 · 5
NumeralsI · IV · V
LyricsLyrics only, no chords
Original keyThe chart as written, without transposing

Chart editor WebiOS

  • Web A full-screen editor with a live preview, a sequence editor, and formatting (font, size, chord color, one or two columns). Choose Download PDF to save it.
  • iOS Edit lyrics and chords with a preview in any key, then Share PDF or Print from the share sheet.

Song book export Web

From any plan, choose Export song book to print every chart in the set, each in its service key.

Rehearsal player

Practice the whole set in order, with the charts in front of you.

A plan's Rehearse tab
  • Plays the plan's recordings in service order, with repeat off, all or one.
  • Change practice speed from 0.5× to 1.25× without changing the pitch.
  • Charts are shown in the service key. External links (for example a video) open in their own player.
  • iOS Background audio keeps playing when the screen is locked or you switch apps.
  • iOS Offline downloads save a plan's rehearsal pack for when there's no signal. Manage them in Settings → Offline downloads.

Messages & chat

Keep conversations next to the plan they're about.

Chat WebiOS

Web: Chat · App: Messages tab → Chat
  • Team conversations and plan conversations. A plan's chat is open to its leaders and the people scheduled on it.
  • @mention people, reply to or quote a message, search, mute a conversation, and delete your own messages.
  • Each plan item also has its own discussion thread.

Messages from leaders WebiOSSchedulers

  • Send a message to everyone on a plan, or to a team. Preview the recipients first.
  • Deliver it in the app, by email, or both. Replies go to the sender's email.
  • Use variables like first name and plan title, and save personal or shared message templates.
  • See Sent and Received lists, with delivery results.

Report & block WebiOS

  1. Tap the picture of the person who wrote a message.
  2. Choose Report message (spam, harassment, inappropriate, safety or other). It goes to your church's moderators.
  3. Or choose Block to hide everything that person writes.

See and unblock people in Settings → Account & privacy on the web, or Settings → Blocked people in the app. Moderators review reports and can hide a message or dismiss the report.

Calendar & sync

See every service at a glance, and put your schedule in the calendar you already use.

Calendar WebiOS

Web: Calendar · App: Calendar tab
  • Month and agenda views. Show Everyone or Only mine, and filter by team.
  • Turn on extra layers for a team: blockouts, birthdays and anniversaries (for people who choose to share them).

Subscribe in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook WebiOS

  1. Go to Settings → Calendar sync.
  2. Create your personal feed link, then copy it or open it in your calendar app. In the app you can also share it.
  3. If the link is ever shared by mistake, rotate it to make a new one and stop the old one.

Web Leaders can also create team and service-type feeds from the Calendar page, with or without rehearsals. Every schedule email includes an .ics file you can add to any calendar.

Notifications & email

Know when you're scheduled, when something changes, and when it's almost time.

Email

  • Serving requests: combined into one email per person, with an .ics attachment.
  • Reminders for unanswered requests and "coming up" reminders for confirmed services.
  • Decline and withdraw notices to schedulers, availability requests, leader messages, invitations and password resets.
  • Emails are written in each person's chosen language.

Push notifications iOS

  • New requests, schedule updates and chat messages. Lock-screen notifications keep details private.
  • The bell icon opens your Notifications feed.
  • In Settings → Notifications: allow push, choose the app-icon badge (unread count or off), pick reminder channels, and mute individual chats.

Reminders

Churches set automatic reminders for unanswered requests and upcoming services. They can be set per church, service type or team: how many hours before, which channels, quiet hours and a daily limit. You choose your own reminder channels under My reminders (web) or Notifications (app).

Profile, language & account

Personal settings that follow you across devices.

  • Profile: photo, name and phone number.
  • Language / Ngôn ngữ: English, Tiếng Việt or Automatic. Emails and push notifications use your choice too. The web app also has a quick language switcher in the header.
  • Appearance Web: light, dark or match your system. The app follows your device setting.

Delete your account WebiOS

Web: Settings → Account & privacy · App: Settings → Delete account

This permanently deletes your account and personal data. Before you confirm, you'll see exactly what will be deleted, including any church where you're the only member.

Own a church with other members? Choose another owner first. You can't delete your account while you're the only owner of a church that still has other people in it.

Plans & order of service

Build each service from songs, headers and items, with times that add themselves up.

Plans list WebiOS

  • Plans are grouped by service type. Filter by Draft, Published or Archived, or show past plans.
  • New plan: title, date and time, service type and location. Needs Edit plans.

The plan workspace WebiOS

Each plan has tabs: Order, Teams, Files, Rehearse, Attendance, plus Stage and Share on the web. From the header you can:

  • Go to the previous or next plan, or add the next plan.
  • Publish or unpublish, Send notifications, or delete the plan.
  • Open Live, the plan chat, or Message people.
  • Web Print this plan and Export song book.

Order of service WebiOSEdit plans

  • Add a Song, Header or Item. Edit, move up or down, or remove items.
  • Give each item a length, notes, a leader, and a segment: Before, During or After the service.
  • For songs, choose the arrangement and the service key, and set a sequence just for this plan.
  • Start times are calculated for every item, with a planned finish time and a warning if the service runs long.
  • Link media to an item, and use each item's notes and discussion.
  • Web Print order: pick a service time, include leaders and choose which notes to print.

Plan notes WebiOS

Add categorized notes and choose who sees each one: everyone, people scheduled, selected teams, or plan leaders only.

Service & rehearsal times WebiOS

  • A plan can have several times: Service, Rehearsal or Other, each with a label (for example "9 AM" and "11 AM").
  • Daylight-saving changes are handled for you.
  • When you move a time, Review time changes shows who's affected and can send them updated requests.

Service types & templates

Set up a recurring service once, then create plans in seconds.

Add a service type Web

  1. Name it (for example "Sunday Morning") and choose how often it happens: weekly, every other week, monthly or occasionally.
  2. Set its usual times.
  3. Pick common teams by category: Music, Technical, Kids, Hospitality or Other. Optionally add yourself to them.

Folders (Settings → Church) group service types by campus or ministry. Permissions given on a folder apply to everything inside it. Default files are added automatically to every new plan of a service type.

Templates WebiOS

  • Web The Templates library holds plan templates and smaller section templates. They can include times, notes, items, needed positions and roster. Older revisions are kept.
  • Web Save as template from any plan.
  • Apply template to a plan, with a preview of the changes before you confirm.

Generate plans WebiOS

Create up to 12 plans at once from a template: choose the first date, repeat every week or every few weeks, set how many (or a stop date), and leave out dates you skip.

Reschedule Web

Move several plans at once, either whole plans or just their order, with a review step and options to reset the roster and notify people.

Scheduling teams

Fill every position, spot problems before you ask, and track every answer.

Schedule people on a plan WebiOSSchedule people

A plan's Teams tab
  • Set the needed positions for each team.
  • Schedule people: pick a team and position, or create a position just for this plan.
  • Add a guest who doesn't have a login, with an optional email invite.
  • Choose which service and rehearsal times each person covers.
  • See warnings for blockouts, double-booking, preferences ("serves too often", avoided dates) and out-of-date availability.
  • Schedule over a conflict only with Schedule over conflicts, and a reason is recorded.
  • See whether each request was delivered, and the plan's delivery history.

Prepare privately, then release WebiOS

Build the schedule without anyone being notified. When you're ready, choose Review and release. On the web, the Release review page shows prepared requests across all plans, so you can release now or at a set time, with or without notifications. Each person gets one combined message.

Staffing matrix Web

See up to 12 plans side by side in a grid. Filter it, save views, select many cells with the keyboard, and prepare or schedule in bulk after a review.

Propose staffing Web

Get an automatic, fair proposal that respects blockouts, conflicts and team rotations. Lock people in, exclude someone or ask for an alternative, then commit the result as prepared requests.

Open positions & sign-ups WebiOS

  • Leaders open a position with a number of spots, a closing time, and a rule: Direct confirmation or Leader approval.
  • Members browse Open positions, sign up and can cancel. Leaders approve or reject sign-ups.

Replacements WebiOS

After declining, choose Ask for a replacement. Leaders send offers, a candidate accepts, and a leader approves. On the web, leaders can also approve swaps between people.

Availability requests Web

Team leaders can ask a team to update their availability by a reply-by date, from Teams → Availability.

People & teams

Your church directory and the teams people serve on.

People WebiOS

Web: People · App: Settings → People
  • Everyone can browse the directory. Contact details are visible only to owners and to the person themselves.
  • Owner Add person and edit their full record: contact details, address, tags, eligibility records and roster history. Archive or restore people.
  • Owner Send or resend an invite with a role, and change a member's role.
  • WebOwner Bulk changes (add or remove tags, add to or remove from a team), tags and groups, join requests and import from CSV.
  • Web Possible duplicate people (Settings → Church): merge records or keep them separate.

Teams WebiOS

  • Web Create teams with a name, description, color and positions (needs Manage teams).
  • Add or remove members: owners, admins and that team's leaders. The app can view teams and change rosters.
  • Web Team settings: team leaders, whether new requests are sent right away or prepared first, secure team (requires an eligibility record, such as a background check), suggest-by-tag rules, which teams can see this roster, and reminders.
  • Roster visibility (Settings → Access): optionally let members see only their own teams' rosters.

Media & files

Keep charts, audio, videos and documents where your team will look for them.

Files WebiOS

Attach files to plans (the Files tab), songs, arrangements, keys, media and service-type defaults.

  • Upload a file, add a link, or write a text document.
  • Categories: chart, lyrics, audio, video, image, document or other.
  • Audience: everyone, people scheduled, selected teams, selected positions, or leaders only.
  • Versions: replace a file with a new version. Plans keep the version they were given.
  • Play-only files can be streamed but not downloaded, for licensed recordings.
  • iOS Add from Files or Photos, or use Scan pages to turn paper into a PDF with the camera.

Media library WebiOS

Web: Media · App: Songs tab → Media

Reusable bundles of videos, cues and graphics, each with a title, length, themes, thumbnail, and usage count. Link them to items in the order of service. Creating and editing bundles is on the web; the app can browse, add files and link media.

Your church's storage usage is shown with a warning before you reach the limit.

Live, stage & run times

Run the service from the plan, and let everyone follow along.

Live WebiOS

  • One live run per service time. The person in control can start, go to the next item, jump, pause, resume and end.
  • Countdown by item length, planned item end or service end, with a projected finish (on time, early or late).
  • Control can be taken, released, or taken over with a reason. Anyone who can see the plan can watch.
  • Run history shows planned vs. actual times. Web Leaders can correct recorded times (changes are logged).
  • Web Stage display: a full-screen confidence view for musicians and speakers.

Stage layouts Web

On a plan's Stage tab, draw where people and gear go on a blank, proscenium, thrust or in-the-round stage. Start from a template, place scheduled people, gear and labels, and get warnings such as "scheduled but not placed".

Public sharing

Share a set list with anyone, without giving them an account.

A plan's Share tab (web, plan editors)
  • Publish a public link and choose exactly what it shows: title, date, times, location, service type, item titles, lengths, song titles, authors and keys.
  • Publish now or on the day of the service. Make a new link or stop sharing at any time.
  • Stable service-type links always point to the next upcoming (or most recent) set list.

Always private: people, notes, files and chat are never included in a public link.

Church settings & access

For owners and admins.

  • Church: name, logo, address, denomination and congregation size.
  • Folders, default files, reminders and duplicate people, described above.
  • Access: open a member to set their role, a scheduler's reach (church-wide or only granted areas), and permissions per folder, service type or team. Check access explains any decision.
  • Delivery: the status of recent emails, push notifications and in-app messages. Filter to problems and Retry.
  • Developer API & webhooks: read-only API keys for published plans, service types and songs, and signed webhooks when a plan is published. Revoke keys at any time.

FAQ

When will the iPhone app be in the App Store?

It's in review with Apple now. The download button on this site will go live as soon as it's approved. Until then, everything works in the web app.

Is there an Android app?

Yes, it's coming soon. It has the same features as the iPhone app, except the Home Screen widget. In the meantime, the web app works in any Android browser.

Do volunteers need an account?

Not to respond. Guests and people without a login can accept or decline from the link in their email. An account lets them see their full schedule, charts, files and chat.

Can I change the key of a song for one service only?

Yes. In the plan's order of service, choose a service key for that song. The chart shows in that key for everyone on the plan, and the song's library key stays the same.

Who can see my phone number and email?

Only church owners and you. Other members see your name and the teams you serve on.

Can someone respond for me?

Yes. Add a helper under Settings → Availability → Help with my schedule. You choose whether they can see requests, answer them or manage your availability, and until when.

Which languages are supported?

English and Vietnamese (Tiếng Việt). Each person picks their own, and emails and notifications follow that choice.

How do I delete my account?

On the web, go to Settings → Account & privacy. In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. See Profile, language & account.
